02 Our Story

Four chairs, one obsession.

Crown & Blade opened in 2012 with two chairs, one barber pole and a simple idea: treat every haircut like it's going on a wedding day. Fourteen years later our team of four master barbers has delivered over 24,000 cuts — and we still argue about the perfect fade line.
